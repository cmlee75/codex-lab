Cook & Bake — Market Validation

A price, audience and launch-readiness check for a hands-on cooking and baking academy with two campuses and classes of 12.

Research current as at 24 September 2026. Competitor prices are published SGD prices accessed on that date.

Summary

There is a real market, but it is segmented: low-cost subsidised training, one-off social/tourist experiences, and premium multi-lesson technical studios. Cook & Bake’s S$160–S$1,580 catalogue belongs primarily in the premium hobbyist segment. Its strongest opening is a small gateway set, then a pathway into four-week technical courses.

Decision: launch five courses, not all 20. Prove paid conversion and class fill before running high-cost, high-trust courses such as sushi, wedding cakes and French foundations.

Personas

Serious hobbyist / micro-business baker

Buys repeatable technical outcomes: sourdough, laminated pastry, macarons and celebration cakes. Wants progression, chef credibility and a portfolio-quality result.

Social experience buyer

Couples, friends, tourists and teams purchasing a memorable shared activity. They favour short, bookable sessions and a meal or take-home outcome.

Household-skills buyer

Parents buying junior skills, and households/employers funding domestic-helper capability. They value practical confidence, safety and reliable home recipes.

Evidence: ABC offers cooking, bread, cake and kids pathways; Commune explicitly targets couples, teams, children and domestic helpers; Palate’s junior series targets ages 10–18. ABC; Commune Kitchen; Palate Sensations. Accessed 24 Sep 2026.

Demand and seasonality

  • December is a demonstrated peak: ABC warns members of longer waits in peak seasons, specifically December.
  • Festive formats are standard: ABC uses seasonal trial menus; Aestheticar sells mooncake, kueh and Zodiac-biscuit workshops.
  • Tourists are a secondary opportunity: STB forecasts 17–18 million international visitor arrivals and S$31.0–32.5 billion in tourism receipts for 2026. This supports short local-food experiences, not eight-week courses.

The five launch courses

  1. Knife Skills & Kitchen Essentials — S$160. Lowest-commitment gateway into every cooking pathway.
  2. Cookie & Biscuit Baking — S$180. Beginner-friendly, giftable and easy to rotate for school-holiday and festive themes.
  3. Thai Street Food Cooking — S$540 / 3. A validated cuisine: Commune sells a comparable Thai workshop at S$185 for one session.
  4. Artisan Sourdough Bread Baking — S$680 / 4. High-intent, repeatable technical skill with a clear home result.
  5. Korean & Asian Bakery — S$720 / 4. Contemporary differentiation while retaining bread-course appeal.

Cook & Bake prices: supplied catalogue, received 24 Sep 2026. Thai comparator: Commune Kitchen, accessed 24 Sep 2026.

Risks

  1. Thin class fill: 20 courses across two campuses can fragment early demand; launch five first and publish a clear minimum-enrolment policy.
  2. Price/value mismatch: S$980–1,580 courses need demonstrable technical progression, chef credentials and a tangible portfolio or career outcome.
  3. Subsidised-price comparison: SkillsFuture/WSQ alternatives create lower visible prices; state clearly whether Cook & Bake is a premium leisure academy, an accredited provider, or both.
