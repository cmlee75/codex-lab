# Cook & Bake — Website Market Brief

## Positioning

Premium, hands-on Singapore cooking and baking academy for people who want practical skills, clear technical progression and memorable food experiences.

## Core personas

1. **Serious hobbyist / micro-business baker:** seeks repeatable skills in sourdough, laminated pastry, macarons and celebration cakes; values progression, chef credibility and a portfolio-quality result.
2. **Social experience buyer:** couples, friends, tourists and teams seeking a bookable shared activity, meal or take-home outcome.
3. **Household-skills buyer:** parents, households and employers buying practical cooking confidence, safety and reliable home recipes for young people or domestic helpers.

## Launch first

1. Knife Skills & Kitchen Essentials — **S$160**, 1 session.
2. Cookie & Biscuit Baking — **S$180**, 1 session.
3. Thai Street Food Cooking — **S$540**, 3 sessions.
4. Artisan Sourdough Bread Baking — **S$680**, 4 sessions.
5. Korean & Asian Bakery — **S$720**, 4 sessions.

## Pricing notes

One-session courses at **S$160–220** are above ABC trial lessons and Food Playground's **S$120–150** experience, but within Commune Kitchen's **S$132–269** public-workshop band. Multi-session Cook & Bake courses price at **S$160–245 per session**: premium against ABC's core bread/cake pathways, but below Creative Culinaire's **S$250/day** Danish and croissant course. High-priced courses require explicit technical progression, chef credentials and a tangible outcome.

## The website must convert

1. Make the five launch courses immediately bookable, showing fee, session count, level, campus, schedule, class size of **12**, take-home result and clear learning outcomes.
2. Use a low-commitment gateway journey: promote Knife Skills and Cookies first, then recommend Thai Street Food, Sourdough or Korean & Asian Bakery as the next course.
3. Make premium value visible: show instructor credibility, hands-on teaching, ingredient/equipment inclusion, outcomes, reviews and a clear minimum-enrolment/cancellation policy.
